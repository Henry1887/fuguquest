## Toolchain notes

- This build has **no `vendor_dlkm` partition**. Modules live in `vendor.img:/lib/modules`.
- `payload-dumper-go` 2.1.0 treats a missing requested partition as fatal and extracts
  nothing. **1.3.0** skips it. Use 1.3.0 for this build.
- `vmlinux-to-elf` installs into the venv; it must be on `PATH` when `port.py` runs.
